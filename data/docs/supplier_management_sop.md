# Standard Operating Procedure: Supplier Management

## 1. Purpose
Defines how suppliers are onboarded, measured, and reviewed so that replenishment lead times and product quality support the inventory policy.

## 2. Supplier Onboarding
1. Procurement collects company details, certifications, and banking information.
2. Quality assurance approves product samples for every category the supplier will serve.
3. The supplier agrees to a standard lead time per category and to the return terms in the Shipping & Logistics Policy.
4. New suppliers start on a 90-day probation with weekly performance check-ins.

## 3. Lead Time Management
- Standard lead time is the agreed number of days from purchase order to receipt at the distribution center.
- Actual lead time is measured for every purchase order and averaged over a rolling 90 days.
- Reorder points use the actual rolling lead time, not the agreed lead time (see the Inventory Management SOP).
- If actual lead time exceeds the agreed lead time by more than 20% for a month, the supplier receives a corrective action request.

## 4. Supplier Scorecard
Suppliers are scored monthly on a 100-point scale:

| Component | Weight | Measure |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| On-time delivery | 40% | Purchase orders received by the agreed date |
| Fill rate | 25% | Units received / units ordered |
| Quality | 25% | 100% minus the defect return rate |
| Responsiveness | 10% | Corrective actions closed on time |

- **Preferred:** 85 points or more
- **Approved:** 70–84 points
- **At risk:** below 70 points for two consecutive months; procurement starts qualifying an alternative supplier

## 5. Quarterly Business Review
Preferred and at-risk suppliers attend a quarterly business review covering the scorecard trend, demand forecast for the next quarter, upcoming product changes, and open corrective actions.

## 6. Supplier Exit
A supplier is exited after two consecutive quarters at risk, a confirmed safety or compliance issue, or by mutual agreement. Open purchase orders are completed or reassigned before exit, and affected categories receive temporary safety stock increases.
