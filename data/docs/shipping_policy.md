# Shipping & Logistics Policy

## 1. Shipping Modes
The following shipping modes are available:

### Standard Shipping
- Transit time: 5-7 business days
- Cost: Lowest tier
- Used for: Category B and C items, non-urgent replenishment

### Second Class
- Transit time: 3-5 business days
- Cost: Mid tier
- Used for: Regular replenishment, Category A items

### First Class
- Transit time: 1-2 business days
- Cost: Premium tier
- Used for: Urgent restocking, critical component shortages

### Same Day
- Transit time: Within 24 hours
- Cost: Emergency tier (requires VP approval for orders > $5,000)
- Used for: Production-line-down scenarios only

## 2. Carrier Selection
- Primary carrier: Selected based on cost and reliability score per route
- Backup carrier: Activated when primary carrier's on-time rate drops below 90%
- Carrier performance is reviewed monthly using delivery data from the warehouse management system

## 3. Late Delivery Tracking
Orders are flagged as "at risk" when:
- Shipping confirmation is not received within 24 hours of order placement
- Tracking shows the shipment has been stationary for 48+ hours
- Weather or logistics disruptions affect the delivery route

## 4. Cost Optimization
- Orders are consolidated where possible to reduce per-unit shipping cost
- The discount rate threshold for free shipping is reviewed quarterly
- Regional warehouse allocation is optimized to minimize average shipping distance

## 5. Returns and Reverse Logistics
- Damaged goods are returned within 30 days for credit
- Return shipping cost is borne by the supplier for quality defects
- Return rate is tracked per category and supplier as a quality signal
